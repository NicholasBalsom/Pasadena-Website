Pasadena

Description

Located at 2A Ryans Road Pasadena, NL

Pasadena Beach sits on the sandy shores of beautiful Deer Lake. Beach Corp Inc. Pasadena Beach is open for family enjoyment throughout the summer. This popular spot is a haven to those who enjoy swimming in the freshwater lake. Those who enjoy more adventurous water sports can explore the miles of waterways on the lake or up or down the world renowned Humber River. Pasadena Beach is especially home to those who just want to spend a lazy summer day with the sand between their toes

The beach is also home to the Oasis Grillhouse so you can enjoy some freshly made food well enjoying your beach paradise

Located at Nordic Drive, Pasadena, NL

The Pasadena Ski Nature Park has over 19 kilometers of groomed ski trails, 2 kilometers of lighted trails for night skiing, 6 kilometers of snowshoeing trails along with a state of the art ski chalet. These wilderness trails features breathtaking views and wildlife of Western Newfoundland. Summer hiking, mountain biking, winter cross-country skiing and snowshoeing in the hills of picturesque Pasadena , The Crown of the Valley , is a beautiful tourist stop any time of the year.

Open all days 9:00 am to 5:00 pm
