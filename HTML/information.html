INFORMATION

N. W. Bennett Sports Field gives 
its visitors a chance to kick back 
for a while and get in touch with 
nature. Use it to do yogasanas, 
participate in celebrations, sunbathe, 
get together with your friends, meet 
some people or play with your kids 
in your free time.

Pasadena Place is a recreation 
complex owned by the Town of 
Pasadena which opened in 2016. 
It includes a modern fitness 
centre, gymnasium with rock 
climbing wall, meeting space 
and several multi-purpose rooms.


Café 59 is a place to sit down, 
relax, and eat delicious foods. 
Such as pizza, garlic fingers, 
schnitzel, salads and sandwiches.
